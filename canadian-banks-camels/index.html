Source. Every series badged OSFI filing is calculated from the banks' regulatory returns to OSFI (BCAR capital, leverage, P3 income statement, E3 impaired loans, M4 monthly balance sheet), published on the Government of Canada Open Government Portal. Figures are on OSFI's reporting basis: reported rather than adjusted, with insurance subsidiaries treated as OSFI requires, so they will not tie exactly to each bank's headline numbers. The LCR series is the one exception: it is not in OSFI's open data and is still from memory, badged unverified. Raw line items behind every ratio are in data/osfi_inputs.csv.

Definitions and regime breaks

  • Build. python3 scripts/build_osfi_data.py downloads the five OSFI files (about 1.2 GB, cached in .cache/osfi) and rewrites data/camels_data.js, data/osfi_camels.csv and data/osfi_inputs.csv. Use --refresh after each OSFI release.
  • Fiscal year. Income items are Q4 year-to-date. Ratios on averages use 13 month-ends (October to October) from the monthly balance sheet.
  • Capital. CET1 from FY2014. OSFI's open data carries only a transitional figure for FY2013, so it is left blank. Tier 1 uses Basel II for FY2009-12. Basel III reforms took effect in Q2 FY2023.
  • IFRS transitions. FY2012 (IFRS adoption) brought securitized mortgages onto the balance sheet, which lifts loans/deposits and lowers liquidity ratios. FY2018 (IFRS 9) moved impaired loans to stage 3 and added performing-loan provisions to PCL.
  • Known distortions. TD FY2023-24 (FHN termination, AML charges) and FY2025 (gain on the Schwab stake sale), BMO FY2022-23 (Bank of the West hedge gains, then losses), NA from FY2018 (Credigy purchased credit-impaired loans in stage 3) and NA from FY2023 (funding costs of trading positions in NII).
  • Sensitivity metrics are derived inside the dashboard from the ROE and PCL series.
